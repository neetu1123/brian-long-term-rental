About the Property

A Rare Stamford
Waterfront Residence

Situated directly on the Long Island Sound, this exceptional Shippan Point residence combines timeless coastal charm with modern luxury living. Expansive windows frame breathtaking water views throughout the home.

At low tide, a private sandy shoreline emerges just steps from the property — ideal for kayaking, paddle boarding, or relaxing beside the water. Only minutes from downtown Stamford and under an hour from Manhattan.

Shippan Point

Stamford's Premier
Waterfront Enclave

Shippan Point is one of the most prestigious waterfront neighborhoods in all of Fairfield County — a gated peninsula jutting into Long Island Sound with unobstructed views of the Connecticut coastline and beyond.

The neighborhood is home to some of the region's most distinguished residences, offering the rare combination of absolute privacy with proximity to world-class dining, culture, and transportation.
